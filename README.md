# 🚀 AgileWell Enterprise

> Human-Centered Intelligence Platform for Agile Teams

AgileWell Enterprise is a web platform designed to support organizations in monitoring, analyzing, and improving the **Human Factors** that influence the performance and well-being of agile software development teams.

This project was developed based on the Master's research:

**Evaluation of Agile Methodologies from a Human Factors Perspective: Physical and Emotional Impacts in the Workplace**

---

## 📌 About the Project

Software development teams often focus on productivity, delivery speed, and project outcomes. However, human factors such as communication, collaboration, workload, mental fatigue, psychological pressure, and autonomy significantly impact both performance and well-being.

AgileWell Enterprise was created to help organizations monitor these factors and promote healthier, more sustainable agile environments.

---

## ✨ Features

### 📊 Squad Analytics
Monitor and compare the health of multiple teams in real time.

- Communication
- Collaboration
- Autonomy
- Role Clarity
- Organizational Support
- Workload
- Mental Fatigue
- Psychological Pressure
- Team Satisfaction

### 🔥 Burnout Detection
Predictive analytics to identify psychosocial risks and early signs of burnout.

### 📖 Integrated Practical Guide
A practical framework that integrates PMBOK and Agile practices with a focus on human well-being.

### 🤖 Agile Coach AI
AI-powered assistant that generates personalized action plans and recommendations for teams and leaders.
